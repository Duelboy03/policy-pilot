# Performance, Growth and Learning

## Performance reviews

We hold formal performance reviews twice a year, in June and December. Each review has three parts: a self-assessment, feedback from peers and a written review from your manager. Ratings use a 1 to 5 scale, where 3 means fully meets expectations. Your manager shares the rating with you in a one-to-one conversation. Calibration meetings between managers make sure ratings are fair across teams.

Managers and employees also meet one-to-one at least every two weeks to talk about priorities and progress, so nothing in a review should be a surprise.

## Pay and promotion

Pay reviews happen once a year, in the December cycle. Promotions can happen in either review cycle. To be considered you need to have performed at the next level for at least two quarters, and your manager nominates you. Promotion decisions are made by a panel of leaders from outside your team.

## Improvement plans

If your performance is below expectations, your manager will discuss it with you early and agree a performance improvement plan. A plan lasts 60 days, with a check-in every two weeks and clear, written goals. HR is involved in every plan.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 a year for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Claim it as an expense with your manager's approval. Attending a conference also needs approval for the time away, which counts as working time, not as vacation.

## Tuition reimbursement

Northwind Labs reimburses up to $5,250 a year of tuition for a degree or certificate program related to your work, paid after you finish each course with a grade of B or better. If you leave within 12 months of a reimbursement you repay it.

## Internal moves

After 12 months in a role you can apply for an internal transfer without telling your manager first. Open roles are posted on the careers page for employees a week before they are advertised externally.

## Mentoring

Anyone can request a mentor from the mentoring program. Matches last six months and are arranged by People Operations.
