# Remote and Hybrid Work

## How we work

Northwind Labs is hybrid by default. Each team agrees its own pattern of office days, and most teams meet in the office two or three days a week. Some roles are fully remote. Your offer letter says which applies to you.

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your own time zone. Be reachable and available for meetings during those hours. Outside them, choose the hours that work for you and your team.

## Home office allowance

Every employee gets a one-time $750 allowance to set up a home workspace, for example a desk, a chair or a monitor. After that, remote employees get $50 a month toward internet and phone costs. Claim both as expenses. Items you buy with the allowance belong to you.

## Company equipment

Everyone receives a laptop, and a headset and monitor on request. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Company equipment stays the property of Northwind Labs and must be returned when you leave. Report a broken or stolen device to IT straight away.

## Working from another location

You can work from anywhere in your home country for up to 20 days a year without approval, for example while visiting family. Working from abroad for any amount of time needs HR approval first, because it can create tax and immigration obligations for you and the company. Ask at least 6 weeks ahead. Approval is not guaranteed and is limited to 30 days.

## Coworking

If your home is not suitable for working, you can use a coworking space. The company pays up to $200 a month with your manager's approval.

## Security when you work remotely

Use the company VPN on public Wi-Fi, lock your screen when you step away, and never let others use your work laptop. See the security policy for details.

## Meetings

Camera use is encouraged but never required. Record decisions in writing so people in other time zones are not left out.
