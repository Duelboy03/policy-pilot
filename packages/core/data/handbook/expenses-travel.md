# Expenses and Business Travel

## Submitting expenses

Pay for business costs with your company card where you have one. Otherwise pay yourself and claim the money back. Submit each claim within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemised receipt for anything over $25. Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.

Approved claims are paid with your next payroll, usually within 5 working days. Reimbursements appear as a separate line on your payslip and are not taxed.

## Approvals

- Up to $500: your manager approves.
- $501 to $2,500: your manager and your department director approve.
- Over $2,500: needs approval in advance from the finance director. Please ask before you buy.

You cannot approve your own expenses, or expenses for your manager.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal at least 14 days before you travel, where you can.

- Flights: economy class. Flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.
- Hotels: up to $250 a night in major cities and $175 elsewhere.
- Meals: a $65 per diem for each full day of travel, no receipts needed. Meals at a client event are claimed separately.
- Ground transport: use public transport or a rideshare. Car rental is allowed for groups of three or more.
- Personal vehicle: mileage is reimbursed at the IRS standard rate.

## What we do not reimburse

Alcohol except at an approved client or team event, personal entertainment, traffic fines, hotel minibar and spa charges, upgrades paid for personal comfort, and travel for a spouse or family.

## Home office and equipment

Home office costs and equipment are covered by the remote work policy. The wellness stipend and the lactation supplies allowance are also claimed as expenses.

## Corporate card

Employees who travel or buy software for the team can request a corporate card from finance. The card is for business use only, and statements must be reconciled by the 5th of each month. Lost cards must be reported immediately.
