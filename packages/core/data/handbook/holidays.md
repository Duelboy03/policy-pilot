# Company Holidays

## Paid holidays

Northwind Labs observes 11 paid company holidays a year:

- New Year's Day, 1 January
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day, third Monday in January
- Presidents' Day, third Monday in February
- Memorial Day, last Monday in May
- Juneteenth, 19 June
- Independence Day, 4 July
- Labor Day, first Monday in September
- Columbus Day, second Monday in October
- Veterans Day, 11 November
- Thanksgiving, fourth Thursday in November
- Christmas Day, 25 December

When a holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed on the Friday before. When it falls on a Sunday it is observed on the Monday after. Ledgerly and the team calendar always show the observed date.

Holidays are not deducted from your vacation. If you take a week of vacation that includes a holiday, only the other working days come out of your balance.

## Floating holiday

Each employee also gets 1 floating holiday a year for a religious, cultural or personal observance that is not on the company calendar, such as Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year or Yom Kippur. Request it in Ledgerly as a personal day with at least 1 working day of notice. The floating holiday does not carry over.

## Holiday pay for hourly employees

Hourly employees are paid their regular daily hours for each company holiday, which counts as hours paid but not as hours worked, so it does not count toward overtime.

## Working on a holiday

Some roles, such as support and operations, need cover on holidays. Employees who work on a company holiday are paid 1.5 times their normal rate for the hours worked and receive another day off in lieu within 60 days. Working on a holiday is always voluntary unless it is written into your role.

## Office closures

The office is closed between Christmas Day and New Year's Day. The days between are not paid holidays, so use vacation, personal days or the floating holiday if you want to be away for the whole period. Only essential support rotas run during the closure.
