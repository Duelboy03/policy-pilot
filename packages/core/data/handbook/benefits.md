# Health, Retirement and Wellbeing Benefits

## Health insurance

Full-time employees can enroll in medical, dental and vision plans. Enrollment must be completed within 30 days of your start date, and coverage begins on the first day of the month after you start. After that, you can only change plans during open enrollment in November or after a qualifying life event such as marriage or the birth of a child, which you must report within 30 days.

Northwind Labs pays 80 percent of the monthly premium for employees and 50 percent for dependents. Your share is deducted from your pay before tax, so it lowers your taxable income.

## Health savings account

If you choose the high-deductible health plan you can open a health savings account. The company puts $1,000 into your account each January, or a prorated amount if you join mid-year. You can add more from your pay up to the annual IRS limit. HSA money is yours and moves with you if you leave.

## 401(k) retirement plan

You can join the 401(k) plan from your first day. Contribute any percentage of your pay, before tax or Roth, up to the IRS annual limit. Northwind Labs matches 50 percent of what you contribute, up to 6 percent of your pay. To get the full match, contribute at least 6 percent. Matching contributions vest immediately, so they are yours from the moment they are paid in.

Your deferral appears on every payslip as "401(k)". You can change your percentage at any time in the benefits portal and the change applies from the next payroll.

## Life and disability insurance

The company pays for basic life insurance equal to 1 times your annual salary and for accidental death cover. Short-term and long-term disability insurance are also company-paid. See the sick leave policy for how short-term disability works.

## Wellbeing

- A wellness stipend of $50 a month for gym memberships, fitness apps or mental health apps, claimed as an expense.
- An employee assistance program, free and confidential, with 8 counselling sessions a year for you and your household.
- A learning and development budget, described in the performance and growth policy.
