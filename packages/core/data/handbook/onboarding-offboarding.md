# Joining and Leaving Northwind Labs

## Before your first day

Your laptop is shipped to you or ready at your desk. HR sends your account details and your onboarding checklist a week ahead. Complete your tax forms, bank details and benefits choices in Ledgerly and the benefits portal in your first week.

## Your first week

On the first day you meet your manager and your onboarding buddy, a colleague who answers the small questions. By the end of the week you should have your accounts, have completed security and conduct training, and have met your team.

## Probation and 30-60-90 day goals

New hires have a 90-day introductory period. Your manager sets goals with you for the first 30, 60 and 90 days and meets you at each point. The introductory period is a chance for both sides to check the fit. It does not change your notice period or benefits, which start on your first day.

## Resigning

Give at least 2 weeks of written notice to your manager and HR. Senior roles, from director up, give 4 weeks. We may ask you to hand over your work during the notice period and may choose to end it earlier, in which case you are paid for the notice period.

## Your last day

- Your final paycheck is paid on your last working day where the law requires it, and otherwise on the next regular pay date. It includes unpaid salary, expenses submitted by then and any vacation payout required by law.
- Health insurance runs to the end of the month in which you leave. You may be able to continue it for up to 18 months under COBRA. HR sends the details within 14 days.
- Your 401(k) stays yours. You can leave it in the plan, roll it into a new employer's plan or move it to an IRA.
- Return all company equipment within 5 working days. Prepaid shipping labels are provided.
- Your accounts are disabled at the end of your last day.

## Exit interview

HR offers every departing employee a confidential exit interview. Taking part is optional, and what you say is used to improve things, not to affect your reference.

## References

We confirm dates of employment and job title for any future employer. A longer reference is given by your manager only if you ask for it.
