# Code of Conduct and Ethics

## Respect at work

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination based on race, colour, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, veteran status or any other protected characteristic are not tolerated, in the office, online or at events.

## Speaking up

If something is wrong, tell us. You can talk to your manager, to HR, or to any senior leader. You can also use the anonymous ethics hotline, which is run by an independent company and is open 24 hours a day. You do not have to give your name.

Northwind Labs does not allow retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or helps with an investigation. Retaliation is a serious breach of this code and can lead to dismissal.

HR acknowledges every report within 2 working days. Investigations are handled confidentially and usually finish within 30 days. You will be told the outcome in as much detail as confidentiality allows.

## Conflicts of interest

Tell your manager and HR about any situation where your personal interests could conflict with the company's, for example a family member who works for a supplier. Outside employment, consulting or board roles need written approval from HR before you start, and cannot be with a competitor.

## Gifts and entertainment

You may accept gifts worth up to $100 a year from any one business contact. Anything above that must be declined or reported to HR. Never accept cash or cash equivalents, and never offer gifts to a public official.

## Confidentiality

Keep confidential information private, both while you work here and afterwards. This covers customer data, financial results, product plans and other people's personal information. Do not share it on social media or with people who do not need it.

## Using company resources

Use company equipment and accounts for company work. Limited personal use is fine if it does not interfere with your job or break the law.

## Breaches

Breaking this code can lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.
