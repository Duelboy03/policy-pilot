# Sick and Medical Leave

## Sick leave allowance

Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days a year, accrued monthly, with a maximum balance of 15 days. Sick leave can be used when you are ill or injured, to attend medical or dental appointments, and to care for a sick child, spouse, parent or other close family member. Mental health days count as sick days. You never need to explain a diagnosis.

## Telling us you are unwell

Let your manager know as early as you can on the first day, ideally before your normal start time. A message is enough. You do not need advance notice for sick leave, unlike vacation. Log the days in Ledgerly when you are back, or ask your manager to do it for you if you are away for longer.

## Doctor's notes

A doctor's note is only required when you are away for more than 3 consecutive working days. Send it to HR, not to your manager, so that medical information stays private.

## Longer illness and the Family and Medical Leave Act

If you or a family member has a serious health condition, you may be eligible for up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave in a 12-month period under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA). To be eligible you must have worked for Northwind Labs for at least 12 months and for at least 1,250 hours in the last 12 months. Your health insurance continues during FMLA leave on the same terms as when you are working.

Ask HR for the paperwork as soon as you know you will need extended leave. We ask for 30 days of notice when the need is foreseeable.

## Short-term disability

If you are unable to work for more than 14 days because of illness, injury or recovery from surgery, short-term disability pays 60 percent of your base salary for up to 12 weeks. Sick leave is used first for the initial 14 days. Short-term disability runs at the same time as FMLA leave.

## Returning to work

When you return from an absence of more than 10 working days, HR will check in about any adjustments that would help, such as a phased return or a temporary change to your hours.
