# Pay, Payroll and Timesheets

## When you are paid

Most employees are paid every second Friday, biweekly, 26 times a year. Leadership roles are paid monthly, on the last business day of the month. Your pay frequency is shown on your profile in Ledgerly. If a pay date falls on a bank holiday, you are paid on the business day before.

Pay is sent by direct deposit. Update your bank details in Ledgerly at least 5 working days before the pay date, otherwise the change applies from the next pay run. Paper checks are not available.

## Payslips

Your payslip appears in Ledgerly on the pay date. It shows your earnings, your pre-tax deductions such as the health premium and your 401(k) deferral, and your taxes, with year-to-date totals.

## Hourly employees and overtime

Hourly employees record their hours in Ledgerly by 10:00 on the Monday after each work week. Overtime is paid at 1.5 times your hourly rate for hours over 40 in a work week, which runs Monday to Sunday. Overtime is calculated for each week separately, so a short week does not cancel out a long one. Overtime must be approved by your manager before you work it, except in an emergency.

Salaried employees classified as exempt are not paid overtime.

## Taxes

Federal income tax is withheld according to your Form W-4. You can update your W-4 at any time. Social Security and Medicare are withheld by law, and Social Security stops once your earnings reach the annual wage base. State tax depends on where you work. Your pre-tax 401(k) deferrals and health premiums reduce your taxable income for income tax, but not for Social Security and Medicare.

## Bonuses

Bonuses are paid with the first pay run after they are approved, and are taxed at the supplemental rate.

## Mistakes

If you think your pay is wrong, tell payroll@northwind.example within 30 days. Underpayments are corrected in the next pay run, or off-cycle within 3 working days if the shortfall is more than $500. Overpayments are recovered over no more than 3 pay periods, and never without telling you first.

## Changes to pay

Raises take effect from the first day of the pay period after they are approved. Unpaid leave and mid-period starts reduce pay for that period in proportion to working days.
