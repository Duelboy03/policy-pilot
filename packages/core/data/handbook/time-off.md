# Paid Time Off

Northwind Labs gives every full-time employee paid time off so that people can rest, travel and look after their lives. This policy covers vacation and personal days. Sick leave and parental leave have their own policies.

## Vacation allowance

Full-time employees receive 20 vacation days per calendar year. Vacation accrues monthly at one twelfth of the annual allowance, which is about 1.67 days a month, and is credited at the end of each month. A new hire starts accruing from the month after they join. Part-time employees accrue in proportion to their scheduled hours.

The maximum vacation balance is 30 days. Once you reach the cap, accrual pauses until you take some time off, so plan your leave before you lose accrual.

## Personal days

Everyone also receives 3 personal days a year, accrued monthly like vacation, with a maximum balance of 5 days. Personal days cover appointments, moving house, religious observances or anything else that does not fit another category. You do not need to give a reason beyond selecting "personal" in the request.

## Requesting time off

Submit every request in Ledgerly. Vacation needs at least 3 working days of notice and personal days need 1 working day. Requests longer than 5 consecutive working days should be raised with your manager at least 4 weeks ahead so the team can plan cover.

Your manager approves or declines within 2 business days. If your manager is away, HR can decide on their behalf. Approved leave appears on the team calendar. You may cancel an approved request any time before it starts. Once leave has started, only HR can change it.

You cannot book more days than you will have accrued by the first day of your leave. The request form shows the balance you will have on that date.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused vacation days carry over into the next calendar year. Days above 5 expire on 31 December. Personal days do not carry over.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave needs 7 days of notice and manager approval. Unpaid days reduce your pay for the period at your daily rate, which is your salary for the period divided by its working days.

## Leaving the company

Unused vacation is paid out when employment ends where the law requires it, and otherwise is not. Personal days are never paid out.
