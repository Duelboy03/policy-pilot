# Parental Leave

Northwind Labs supports employees becoming parents by birth, adoption or foster placement.

## How much leave you get

Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. This is in addition to any medical recovery time covered by short-term disability. Non-birth parents, adoptive parents and foster parents receive 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The same amount applies to each parent when both work at Northwind Labs. Parental leave is available from your first day of employment.

Leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks, and must be used within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Telling us

Please tell your manager and HR at least 30 days before you plan to start leave, or as soon as you can if the date is unexpected, for example an early birth or a short-notice adoption placement. HR will confirm your dates in writing and set up your leave in the payroll system so your pay continues as normal.

## Benefits during leave

Your health, dental and vision coverage continues on the same terms, and we keep paying the employer share. Your 401(k) match continues on the pay you receive. Vacation and sick leave keep accruing while you are on parental leave.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from parental leave can use a phased return. For the first 4 weeks you can work 80 percent of your normal hours at 100 percent of your pay. Agree the schedule with your manager before you return.

## Lactation support

Private lactation rooms are available in every office, with a refrigerator and a sink. Remote employees can claim up to $300 a year toward a breast pump or related supplies as an expense. Take the time you need during the day. You do not need to make up the time.

## Other family leave

Employees can take up to 3 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member and 1 day for other relatives or close friends. Bereavement leave does not reduce your sick or vacation balance.
